Wholesale Christmas Tree Buyer FAQ

Answers from Guangzhou Xiaoyu Import & Export Co., Ltd. for global B2B buyers.

What Christmas products do you supply?

Artificial full-size, slim, pencil, desktop, pre-lit, flocked and specialty Christmas trees, plus ornaments, LED lights and decoration kits.

Do you offer OEM, ODM and private label?

Yes. Custom specifications, colors, lighting, branding and retail or bulk packaging are available. Feasibility and MOQ depend on the requested design.

What is the minimum order quantity?

MOQ varies by product, material, size and customization. Standard models are generally more flexible; custom colors, molds and packaging may require higher quantities.

Can I order a sample?

Sample availability, cost and delivery time depend on the model. Send the target specification and destination for confirmation.

How long does production take?

Lead time depends on product mix, quantity, customization and seasonal capacity. A current schedule is provided after specifications are confirmed.

How is quality controlled?

Quality control can cover approved samples, dimensions, appearance, stability, lighting functions, packaging and carton marks. Destination-market compliance requirements should be specified before production.

Can you ship internationally?

Yes. Export documentation and shipping by sea, air or express can be arranged based on shipment volume, destination and timing.
