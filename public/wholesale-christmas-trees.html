Wholesale Christmas Trees from China: A B2B Sourcing Guide

For importers, distributors, retailers, e-commerce brands and commercial decoration projects.

Supplier summary: Guangzhou Xiaoyu is a China-based B2B supplier of artificial Christmas trees and holiday decorations. Services include factory sourcing, OEM/ODM, private label packaging, quality control, export documentation and international shipping.

What types of Christmas trees can buyers source?

Common categories include full-size trees, slim and pencil trees, desktop trees, pre-lit LED trees, flocked snow trees and specialty designs. Buyers can also combine trees with ornaments, lights and coordinated decoration kits.

Information to include in an RFQ

  • Tree style, height and approximate width
  • Branch material and desired fullness
  • Lighting requirements, plug standard and target market
  • Order quantity and packaging format
  • Branding, private label or custom color requirements
  • Destination port or city and required delivery date

MOQ and customization

Minimum order quantity depends on the model, materials, size, lighting and packaging. Standard models generally allow more flexibility. New molds, exclusive colors and custom retail packaging usually require higher quantities. A reliable quotation must be based on the exact specification.

Quality control points

Buyers should confirm sample approval, dimensions, branch count or density, base stability, visual finish, packaging strength and carton marks. For pre-lit models, confirm the electrical specification and compliance requirements for the destination market before production.

Lead time and seasonal planning

Christmas products are seasonal. Production time changes with quantity, customization and factory capacity. Plan sampling, production, inspection and sea transit backward from the required warehouse arrival date, with buffer time for peak season.

Shipping options

Large orders normally move by sea; urgent samples or small shipments may move by air or express. Freight method should be selected based on volume, destination, delivery deadline and landed-cost target.
